## 2. Apollo Lunar Modules (Moon) — one journey per landing

Per your decision, each landing is its own journey. The data below covers all six landings so that representative ones can be chosen; I recommend starting with **Apollo 11 (Eagle)** and **Apollo 15 (Falcon)** because both have complete NSSDCA records and Apollo 15 also connects to the rover and ALSEP journeys.

### 2.1 What the Lunar Module was (LM-11)

- ✅ Eagle was "the first crewed vehicle to land on the Moon", a two-stage vehicle.
- ✅ **Descent stage:** octagonal prism 4.2 m across and 1.7 m thick; it "served as a platform for launching the ascent stage and was left behind on the Moon."
- ✅ **Ascent stage:** about 2.8 m high; it "returned the astronauts to the CSM" (the command-and-service module in lunar orbit).
- ✅ Both stages together with propellant: 15,103 kg.
- Why the descent stage stays: ✅ it was the launch platform for the ascent stage (LM-11). Any further reasoning (e.g. weight) is ❓ not stated; do not add it.

### 2.3 What happened to each Lunar Module

Every NSSDCA page states the descent stage was "left behind on the Moon". None gives its resting coordinates (❓), but the landing coordinates above are the landing site. The **ascent stages had different endings**, which is exactly why the project must not call everything "abandoned":

| Mission | Ascent stage fate (as stated) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Apollo 11 | Jettisoned into lunar orbit 22 Jul 1969, 00:01:01 UT. "The fate of the LM is not known, but it is assumed that it crashed into the lunar surface sometime within the following 1 to 4 months." | LM-11 |
| Apollo 12 | Jettisoned and "intentionally crashed into the Moon" about 60 km from the Apollo 12 seismic station (3.94° S, 338.80° E) | LM-12 |
| Apollo 14 | Jettisoned 22:48:00 UT and impacted the Moon at 3.42° S, 19.67° W at 00:45:25 UT on 7 Feb 1971 | LM-14 |
| Apollo 15 | Jettisoned 3 Aug 1971, 01:04:14 UT; impacted at 26.36° N, 0.25° E, 03:03:37 UT | LM-15 |
| Apollo 16 | Planned impact was impossible after loss of attitude control, so it "was left in lunar orbit with an estimated life of 1 year" | LM-16 |
| Apollo 17 | Jettisoned 15 Dec 1972, 04:51:31 UT; "fired into the Moon"; struck at 19.97° N, 30.49° E, 06:50:21 UT | LM-17 |

❓ Not stated: the Apollo 12 impact date, and any current status of Apollo 16's ascent stage beyond the one-year estimate.

**Story notes**
- Pioneer 10 is **not** abandoned or broken: its mission was a planned Jupiter flyby, it kept sending data for decades, and it went quiet when its nuclear power source faded.
- Distinguish three different dates: routine mission ended (1997, budget), last signal (23 Jan 2003), and last attempt (Feb 2003 / Mar 2006).
