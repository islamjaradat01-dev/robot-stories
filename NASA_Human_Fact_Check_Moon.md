## 1. Cross-mission findings (these affect several missions)

1. **Facts cite a source page that does not contain them.** The numbers are right but the citation is wrong. Examples: Apollo 11 launch time, pad and splashdown are on CSM11, not LM11; Surveyor 3 and 6 launch times, pad, picture counts and sampler specifications are on S3/S6, not on the NSSDCA overview (SURV, which I searched and found none of them); the ALSEP coordinates are cited to SITES but use LM-page values. Each case is listed in the mission sections.
2. **Two ALSEP errors are repeated in both ALSEP missions and in Apollo 15 Falcon's research**, because they started in R2: "about 70 watts **at launch**" (NASA says only "approximately 70 W DC at 16 V. (63.5 W after one year.)") and "a fuel capsule that **held about 4 kg of plutonium-238**" (NASA: "an approximately 4 kg fuel capsule… which contained plutonium-238": the capsule weighs about 4 kg; the amount of plutonium is not stated).
3. **NASA-versus-NASA conflicts the data does not record:** the Apollo 17 rover distance (three different totals), the Apollo 11 ascent-stage jettison time, the Surveyor 3 landing date, the Surveyor 6 landing position, and small coordinate differences for the ALSEP sites.
4. **Several "NOT STATED" notes are now out of date.** NASA does say: when the Apollo 12 ascent stage hit the Moon; where the Lunar Modules sit (coordinates determined from LRO images); why Surveyor 6 went into hibernation and that contact was briefly regained on 14 December 1967; what the Surveyor 3 last-contact date followed ("two days after the lunar night began"); and that the rover TV cameras were still working at liftoff.
5. **Real NASA evidence found for items we had called unknowable** (all quoted in the sections below): the NSSDCA page that says the Apollo Lunar Modules "were deliberately targeted to impact the Moon"; the NASA caption that the Apollo 12 seismometer "registered" the ascent-stage impact; the rover TV camera statements for Apollo 15, 16 and 17.
6. **Arabic terminology varies between missions** for the same thing: "مرحلة الصعود" (Apollo 11, Falcon) versus "المرحلة الصاعدة" (ALSEP missions); "مركبة جوالة" versus "عربة قمرية". Harmonize.
7. **Research files that need a later correction (not edited now):** R2 (ALSEP "at launch"/"4 kg plutonium"), R1 (Apollo 12 ascent-stage impact date "NOT STATED"), R3 section 1 (the LRV gap is partly closed, see the LRV sections).
8. **Not readable by me:** the NSSDCA "ALSEP Termination Report" PDF (https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/misc/documents/b32116.pdf) is a scanned image with no text. It may state why ALSEP stations were shut down in 1977. A human should read it; until then the reason stays NOT STATED.
